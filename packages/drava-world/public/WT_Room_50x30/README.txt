WHITE TUSKER — FOUR-SCREEN EXPERIENCE ROOM

DIMENSIONS
Room shell: 50 ft wide x 30 ft deep x 14 ft high
Metric: 15.240 m x 9.144 m x 4.2672 m
Front wall spans 50 ft. Side walls span 30 ft. Units in the GLB are metres.
Wall thickness is outside the nominal interior shell; panels and fixtures project
inwards. An 8 ft wide x 9 ft high rear entry is included for access.

SCREEN LAYOUT
- One large 16:9 active display on the left wall.
- Two large 16:9 active displays side by side on the front wall.
- One large 16:9 active display on the right wall.
Each wall display is 16 ft wide x 9 ft high. This size was selected to suit the
requested room. The front displays have a 4 ft gap between their active areas.
All four have dark slim housings and separate, named screen surfaces with
0-to-1 texture coordinates for future media replacement.

CENTRAL KIOSK
True 55-inch diagonal, landscape 16:9 active panel:
1217.591 mm wide x 684.895 mm high.
Screen surface: 45 degrees from horizontal, facing the rear entry.
Pedestal and base: centred on the room's X/Z origin.
Reference-inspired rounded light metallic housing, graphite pedestal face,
light metallic base and a support shaped to meet the sloping screen underside.
The screen is neutral and luminous, ready for your content.

SPACE TREATMENT
Crimson metallic front and left wall panels. Gloss graphite right and rear
walls, polished dark floor, silver White Tusker logo between the front screens.
Dark ceiling with a 20 ft circular diffuser and continuous warm perimeter cove.
The ceiling shell is 14 ft high; mounted lighting and inset ceiling sit below it.
Finishes are matched to the previously delivered glossy WTZ1 material family.

LIGHTING AND VIEWER BEHAVIOUR
The GLBs contain PBR metallic/roughness, normal, AO and clearcoat materials.
Direct diffuse lighting and two sampled diffuse colour-bounce iterations are
baked into embedded emissive maps. This preserves static lighting in portable
glTF while allowing specular response from the destination viewer.
Shine and reflections remain view dependent; use environment lighting in your
GLB viewer. Viewer lighting and tone mapping affect the final appearance.
The supplied previews are CPU ray-traced studies of the model geometry and
materials, not screenshots of a particular web viewer or a 3ds Max render.
This is a concept model based on the references, not fabrication/shop drawings.
