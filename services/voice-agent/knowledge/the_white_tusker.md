## Who we are

The White Tusker is a brand experience design studio. We are experience architects: we curate and orchestrate experiences. We call ourselves a "story-engine" rather than a traditional service company.

What we do in one line: we design spaces that respond to people, content, data and events.

Our one external proposition: design, connect, create and control the complete experience. Our four internal verticals support it.

Tagline: "Fluid by Nature. Adaptive by Design."

Core belief: we design experiences that live beyond screens and walls. Experiences people walk into, interact with, and remember.

Approach: space is language, technology is a tool, and story is the primary driver. We build interconnected systems instead of isolated deliverables, with no silos between disciplines.

## Our four internal verticals

1. Architecture and interior spaces: environmental design that communicates brand intent through spatial narrative, material, light and technology.
2. Immersive and interactive technology: custom interactive installations, CMS platforms, immersive environments and digital twins built for participation.
3. Brand solutions: identity systems, logos, brand books and spatial branding.
4. Corporate and brand films: strategy-led storytelling, scripting, production and post-production for corporate, product and narrative films.
