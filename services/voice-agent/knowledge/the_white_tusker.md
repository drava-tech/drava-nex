# White Tusker

## Who we are

<!-- say: who we are -->

White Tusker is a brand experience design studio. We are experience architects who curate and orchestrate experiences, a "story-engine" rather than a traditional service company.

In one line: we design spaces that respond to people, content, data and events.

Our proposition: design, connect, create and control the complete experience.

Tagline: "Fluid by Nature. Adaptive by Design."

Approach: space is language, technology is a tool, and story is the primary driver. We build connected systems, not isolated deliverables, with no silos between disciplines.

## Our three verticals

<!-- say: what we do -->

1. Architecture and interior spaces: spatial design that communicates brand intent through material, light and technology.
2. Immersive and interactive technology: interactive installations, CMS platforms, immersive environments and digital twins.
3. Brand solutions: identity systems, logos, brand books, spatial branding, and corporate and brand films.

## Clients

<!-- say: our clients -->

Boeing, Collins Aerospace, Bosch, Volvo, Tata Steel, DIAGEO, Infosys, Tech Mahindra, NTT Data, PETRONAS, Herbalife, Embassy and others across technology, aerospace, automotive and corporate sectors.

## Projects

<!-- say: our projects -->

Our flagship is the Collins Aerospace campus in Bengaluru: an experience centre that takes visitors through a flight from check-in to boarding, plus the façade, arrival gate, courtyard, wayfinding and facility branding. For Tata Steel we built a digital twin of the Jamshedpur plant with real-time data. We have also designed experience centres for Boeing, Volvo, Bosch India, Lamborghini Super Trofeo, Johnnie Walker and NTT Data, and made films for Tanishq, Titan x Ducati and SAP.

## Founders and leadership

<!-- say: our founders -->

White Tusker was founded by Smriti Attavar, Naveen DC and Varun Parikh. All three are directors of the studio and together lead its three verticals: architecture and interiors, immersive technology, and brand solutions.

## Team

<!-- say: our team -->

Our team brings architects, interior designers, developers, 3D and motion artists, filmmakers and brand designers together in one studio in Indiranagar, Bengaluru. Nobody works in a silo: one project can move from spatial design to custom software to a brand film, carried by the same team. Our culture starts with one habit: we don't just follow briefs, we question them.

## How we work

<!-- say: how we work -->

We take projects from brief to install under one roof. We start by questioning the brief to find the story, then design the space, content and technology together as one narrative. We handle detailing, fabrication and on-site installation ourselves, so the result matches the concept.
